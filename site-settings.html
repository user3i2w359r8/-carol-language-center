The tool updates the page metadata, language alternates, structured business information, email links, sitemap and discovery files together.

Extract the new ZIP into a new folder, then replace the website files on your host when you are ready. This tool does not publish anything or change your original folder.

Need to change something else?

Contact details live in business-profile.json. Course copy lives in site-content.js. The optional build-site.mjs script rebuilds the static pages after those edits. See README.txt for the complete instructions.

Adding the website address generates real canonical URLs, reciprocal English/Arabic language tags and a populated sitemap. Until then, those URL-dependent fields are intentionally left unset.
