Your language, your reason

Take your curiosity a little further.

Turkish may sound familiar from films, music or a visit to Türkiye. Learning the language gives that interest a new direction: reading signs, asking a question, understanding a reply or getting to know someone beyond a greeting.

The Turkish alphabet and sentence patterns may be new to you, and that is useful to mention when you enquire. If you have studied before, tell Carol what you already know. Discuss your goal, availability and the current timetable and fees with the center before choosing a course.

Before you begin

A few useful
answers.

Do I need to know Turkish before asking about a course?

No. Let Carol know if Turkish is completely new to you, or describe any previous study. Ask about the current course options for your starting point and confirm the schedule and fees directly with the center.

Where is the center?

You can find Carol on Beshara Khoury Street in Tripoli, Lebanon, next to KFC, in the same building as Boulevard Pharmacy. The map and directions link below show the location.

What are the course fees and schedules?

Fees and schedules depend on the course options currently available. Ask Carol directly about your chosen language, starting point, timetable and total cost before registering.
