Your language, your reason

Make Italian part of your story.

Sometimes a language begins with something that catches your attention: a song, a film, a place or a conversation you wish you could follow. Italian gives that curiosity somewhere to go.

Think about what brought you to the language and whether you have studied it before. Sharing your interests helps make your first conversation with Carol more useful when you ask about the available course options.

Your first message

A little context goes a long way.

When you contact Carol, it helps to include:

  1. The language you want to learn.
  2. Any experience you already have.
  3. What you would like to use it for.

Before you begin

A few useful
answers.

Can I ask about Italian if I have never studied it?

Yes. Tell Carol that Italian is new to you and explain why you would like to learn it. Ask about the course options available for your starting point before deciding what suits you.

Where is the center?

You can find Carol on Beshara Khoury Street in Tripoli, Lebanon, next to KFC, in the same building as Boulevard Pharmacy. The map and directions link below show the location.

What are the course fees and schedules?

Fees and schedules depend on the course options currently available. Ask Carol directly about your chosen language, starting point, timetable and total cost before registering.
