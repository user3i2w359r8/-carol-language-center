Choosing a course

Your reason is
a good place to start.

You might want to connect with people, follow an interest, support your studies or feel more comfortable with a language you have already met. Bring that reason with you when you contact Carol.

Tell us which language interests you and whether you have studied it before. Course options and visiting arrangements are discussed directly with the center, so you can get an answer that fits your enquiry.

Your first message

A little context goes a long way.
