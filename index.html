Ask Carol
Which languages can I learn at Carol?

Carol for Language Courses offers German, English, Italian, Spanish, French and Turkish. Explore each language page, or contact the center if you would like help choosing where to begin.

Where is the center?

You can find Carol on Beshara Khoury Street in Tripoli, Lebanon, next to KFC, in the same building as Boulevard Pharmacy. The map and directions link below show the location.

How do I ask about a course?

Send a WhatsApp message to +961 78 957 735 or call the center. Mention the language you want to learn, any previous experience and your goal. Carol will explain the available options.

What should I do before visiting?

Contact the center before making the trip so you can arrange a suitable time and confirm the visiting details.

What are the course fees and schedules?

Fees and schedules depend on the course options currently available. Ask Carol directly about your chosen language, starting point, timetable and total cost before registering.
