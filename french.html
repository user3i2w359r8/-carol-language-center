Your language, your reason

Make room for French in your day.

In Lebanon, French can be part of a conversation, a university text, a message at work or a film you enjoy. Start with the situations that matter to you: finding the right words, following a discussion or understanding what you read.

When you ask about a French course, tell us what you have studied before and what feels difficult today. You might be starting from the beginning, returning after a break or looking to use what you already know. The center can explain the available course options, fees and schedule directly.

Before you begin

A few useful
answers.

Can I ask about French if I studied it at school?

Of course. Tell Carol what you remember, what you find difficult and what you want to use French for now. Ask which current course options suit your starting point, and confirm the timetable and fees before registering.

Where is the center?

You can find Carol on Beshara Khoury Street in Tripoli, Lebanon, next to KFC, in the same building as Boulevard Pharmacy. The map and directions link below show the location.

What are the course fees and schedules?

Fees and schedules depend on the course options currently available. Ask Carol directly about your chosen language, starting point, timetable and total cost before registering.
