Your language, your reason

Give your German a purpose.

Learning a language becomes more personal when you know why it matters to you. You might want to introduce yourself, understand an everyday conversation or feel more at ease when travelling.

Before choosing a course, think about your starting point. Have you studied German before? What do you already feel comfortable doing? Share that with Carol so you can ask about the most suitable next step.

What should I mention when asking about German?

Tell us whether you have studied German before and what you want to use it for. You can also mention the days that usually work for you. Carol can then explain the course options currently available.

Where is the center?

You can find Carol on Beshara Khoury Street in Tripoli, Lebanon, next to KFC, in the same building as Boulevard Pharmacy. The map and directions link below show the location.

What are the course fees and schedules?

Fees and schedules depend on the course options currently available. Ask Carol directly about your chosen language, starting point, timetable and total cost before registering.
