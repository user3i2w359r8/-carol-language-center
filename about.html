About Carol for Language Courses

A language center.
A personal welcome.

A language tutoring center in Tripoli, Lebanon, with a simple belief: learning starts with connection.

Welcome to Carol

Bring your curiosity.
Be yourself.

Carol for Language Courses offers German, English, Italian, Spanish, French and Turkish in Tripoli. At the center is a warm, familiar atmosphere: the kind of place where learning a new language can begin with simply feeling welcome.

Your reason to learn may be personal, practical or still taking shape. A conversation about your interests and experience is the starting point for finding out what the center can offer you.
